How the shading works

At each tilt, the safe pan range is linearly interpolated between the two nearest breakpoints (flat beyond the outermost ones) — the same model planned for target_allowed() in controller.py and pantilt_program.validate_points(), so this preview matches what the real restriction will enforce once real numbers are in.
